## 1 Essay 类型 (Agree or Disagree)

**Essay topic**

Some people believe law changes human behaviour. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

**WE 学习资料具体示范_1**

Nowadays, whether **<u>the law changes human behaviour</u>** has attracted numerous controversies and drawn the public's attention. Some people believe that **<u>the law can change human behaviour</u>**, while others hold the view that **<u>there is no relationship between law and human behaviour</u>**. In my opinion, **<u>the law does have an influence on human behaviour for social and psychological reasons</u>**.

First and foremost, the **<u>social reason</u>** is why I think **<u>the law has an influence on human behaviour</u>**. Although potentially **<u>negative impacts of law</u>** must be taken into account, I still reckon that **<u>the existence of law</u>** has in many ways enormously **<u>enriched</u>** people's lives. For example, a well-known American scientist Einstein once said that **<u>people are generally afraid of receiving any form of legal punishment</u>**. It is thus clearly shown that **<u>human behaviour is changed by law</u>**.

In addition, another factor that should be considered is the **<u>psychological reason</u>**. As far as society is concerned, the ultimate aim of **<u>the law is to protect people</u>**. For instance, a recent article published by The Economist reveals that **<u>80% of people fasten their seat-belts in cars after a traffic law has been enacted</u>**. Undoubtedly, this is a clear indication that **<u>the law has a positive impact on individuals' behaviour</u>**.

In conclusion, I hold the view that **<u>the law changes human behaviour</u>**. To address this issue, the government and society should make a concerted effort to find solutions.

## 2 Essay类型 (两个问题)

**Essay topic**

As a result of advances in medical care, average life expectancy is increasing for men and women. Do you think most people will see this as a positive development? What are the disadvantages of an ageing population for individuals and society? Support your point of view with reasons and/or examples from your own experience or observations.

**WE 学习资料具体示范_2**

Nowadays, whether **<u>increasing life expectancy is a positive development</u>** has attracted numerous controversies and drawn the public's attention. Some people believe that **<u>living longer is very beneficial</u>**, while others hold the view that **<u>it is very harmful</u>**. In my opinion, **<u>increasing life expectancy has an advantage and a disadvantage for social and financial reasons</u>**.

First and foremost, the **<u>social</u>** reason is why I think **<u>living longer is beneficial</u>**. Although potentially **<u>negative impacts of increasing life expectancy</u>** must be taken into account, I still reckon that **<u>long life expectancy</u>** has in many ways enormously **<u>enriched</u>** people's lives. For example, a well-known American scientist Einstein once said that **<u>living longer will allow people to accompany their families longer</u>**. It is thus clearly shown that **<u>increasing life expectancy is great for society</u>**.

Moreover, another factor that should be considered is the **<u>financial</u>** reason. As far as society is concerned, the ultimate aim of **<u>families</u>** is to **<u>support the elderly</u>**. For instance, a recent article published by Scientific American revealed that **<u>families have great financial pressure in supporting elderly people</u>**. Undoubtedly, this is a clear indication that **<u>the ageing population does have some negative influences on many families</u>**.

To sum up, I hold the view that **<u>increasing life expectancy is certainly a positive development</u>**. However, **<u>it also has the disadvantage of financial pressure</u>**. To address this issue, the government and society should make a concerted effort to find solutions.

## 3 Essay 类型 (开放式)

**Essay topic**

Culture shock is a new worry leading to less immigration. What are the possible options to eradicate this issue?

**WE 学习资料具体示范_3**

Nowadays, **<u>solving the problem of culture shock</u>**, which is a new worry leading to **<u>less immigration</u>**, has attracted numerous controversies and drawn the public's attention. Some people believe that **<u>people should travel more</u>**, while others hold the view that **<u>there should be more events hosted to bridge the gap between people from different cultures</u>**. In my opinion, **<u>this problem can be solved by individuals and governments working together</u>**.

First and foremost, **<u>governments need to host more events for people from different cultures</u>**. Although potentially **<u>negative impacts of community events</u>** must be taken into account, I still reckon that **<u>community events</u>** have in many ways enormously **<u>enriched</u>** people's lives. For example, a well-known American scientist Einstein once said that **<u>people will have a better understanding of each other if they spend more time together</u>**. It is thus clearly shown that **<u>culture shock can be reduced if people understand other cultures better through community events</u>**.

Moreover, another factor that should be considered is that **<u>individuals should travel more</u>**. As far as society is concerned, the ultimate aim of **<u>travel</u>** is to **<u>experience the unknown</u>**. For instance, a recent article published in Scientific American revealed that **<u>80% of people are more willing to accept new cultures after travel</u>**. Undoubtedly, this is a clear indication that **<u>culture shock will become less of a problem if people travel more</u>**.

In conclusion, I hold the view that **<u>culture shock can be managed by both individuals and governments</u>**. To address this issue, the government and society should make a concerted effort to bridge the gap between different cultures.
