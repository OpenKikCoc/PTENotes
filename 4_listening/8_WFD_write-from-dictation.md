> [!TIP] **练习方法**
> 
> 正确练习方法 (循环输入法, 10~20个一组)
> 1. 循环听 听懂 (每个单词的意思+了解句子结构+听懂整句)
> 2. 复述 推动理解

> [!TIP] **加词策略**
>
> 1. 救命词汇(高频出现)
>    the of to is a in and are for be students
>    冠词 the a
>    be动词 be is are
>    连词 and
>    介词 of to in for
>    名次 students
> 2. 所有名次的单复数形式
>    finding findings, chocolate chocoloates, memories, mood moods
> 3. 动词时态
> 4. 相似音辨析
>    input/improve, this/these, their/there/they're, you're/you, its/it's, to/too/two, it is/it has,

> [!TIP] **容易听混的**
>
> a an the 同时写
> of | in on for at | that | their this
