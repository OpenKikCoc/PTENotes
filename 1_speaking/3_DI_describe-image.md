## DI 整体答题原则

1. 答题要言之有物
2. 不要说和题目内容毫不相关的废话
3. 不要用过度重复的句式

> 不低于20s; 语法错误不用太担心

猩际新 DI 答题框架分成 3 个大类（数据图，流程图，图片题）

## 1 数据图答题框架

**开头明确主题的句式（一句话）**

- The graph shows us the ...（填入图片标题/主题）
- This image provides information about ...（填入图片标题/主题）
- The graph outlines the ...（填入图片标题/主题）

**中间阐述图片信息，数据图的描述方向（至少选择 2 个不同方向，总共 4-5 句话进行描述）**

1. 描述单个点
2. 比较 2 个点的大小
3. 描述最大/最小

**描述单个点的句式**

- It seems that the figure of … is …
- Based on the graph, it can be found that the value of ... is about...
- It can be seen from the graph that the value of ... is nearly ...
- According to the graph, the value of ... appears to be ...

**比较 2 个点的大小的句式**

- From the graph, it can be seen that the number of ... is around ..., which is greater/smaller.
- The graph reveals that the number of ... is around ..., which is higher/lower.
- Looking at the graph, I notice the number of ... is about ..., which is greater/smaller.

**描述最大最小**

- The highest value of ... is observed in ..., which is ...
- The largest amount of ... is displayed in ..., which is ...
- The smallest value of ... is found in ..., with a total of ...
- The least amount of ... can be seen in ..., with a sum of ...

**结尾概括说明整体情况（一句话）**

可以描述整体趋势或者点明哪个部分数据更大一些，如果觉得这些对你还是比较困难，至少可以再次点明讨论主题（切记一定不要说无意义的套话，比如 In conclusion, this graph is very informative 就不要再说了）：

1. 整体趋势
   - Overall, the graph shows an increase/decrease in...
   - To sum up, the data shows a steady rise/fall in...
   - In summary, we can see from the graph that ... is going up/down.

2. 不同类别，哪个整体更大一些
   - Overall, … has a greater value.
   - The data clearly shows that …accounts for a larger part.
   - In summary, … has a higher figure.

3. 再次点明图片主题
   - In summary, this graph shows details about...
   - In conclusion, this graph provides information about...
   - Overall, this graph outlines...

## 2 流程图答题框架

流程图的开头和数据图一样，开头明确主题（一句话），这里依然可以套用数据图的开头句式。

针对流程图的 body 部分，我们主要呈现的是各步骤的顺序以及每个步骤的名称等，尤其是第一步和最后一个步骤一定要点到。注意一定要按照步骤的顺序来。通常涉及到4-5个步骤，可以根据图片的不同增减步骤的表达。

**关于第一步的表达**

- The first step is …
- The graph shows that the first step is...
- The initial step in this graph is...
- Looking at the graph, the first step is...

**关于中间步骤的表达**

- Moving on, the second step is ...
- Furthermore, the second step shows ...
- The graph also shows that the third step is ...
- In addition, the third step can be seen as ...
- The next stage in the process is the fourth step, which involves ...
- Then, the fourth step requires ...
- After ..., the next stage is the …
- The following step moves to ...
- After that, the process moves to ...
- The next action is to move to ...
- The following step leads to ...
- The final step is ...
- To finish, the last step is ...
- The last step involves ...

**流程图的最后**

可以像数据图一样，再次点明图片主题：

- In summary, this graph shows details about...
- In conclusion, this graph provides information about...
- Overall, this graph outlines...

**流程图示范**

**[图片位置2：流程图示范 - 显示供应链管理流程]**

```text
The graph shows us the supply chain management.

Looking at the graph, the first step is raw materials.

Moving on, the second step is components.

In addition, the third step can be seen as manufacturers.

After that, the process moves to retailer.

To finish, the last step is consumer.

Overall, this graph outlines supply chain management.
```

## 3 图片题答题框架

图片题的话不用特别担心内容的呈现是不是非常有逻辑，因为我们在描述图片时本身就是有些主观的，也没有什么所谓的标准答案，在描述时我们往往可以从图片中物体的特征入手。

开头依然是像数据图或者流程图一样，一句话明确主题。

中间阐述图片信息，图片的描述方向通常为：图片中的主要元素或物体的方位以及他们的特征，这些特征通常包括物体的颜色、大小、形状等，请至少描述3-4个不同方位的信息。

**中间**

- In the middle of the graph, I notice the 颜色/大小/形状 of ... is...
- The graph shows that, in the middle, the 颜色/大小/形状 of ... is...
- I observe that the 颜色/大小/形状 of ...is ...in the middle of the graph.

**左边**

- To the left of the graph, the 颜色/大小/形状 of ... is ....
- On the left side of the graph, I notice the 颜色/大小/形状 of ... is ...
- On the left part of the graph, we find that the .颜色/大小/形状 of ... is ...

**右边**

- What's more, we can see from the graph that, at the right corner, there is ..., and the 颜色/大小/形状 is ...
- Additionally, the graph shows that in the right part, we see..., and its 颜色/大小/形状 is ...
- The graph also reveals that at the right corner, ... is present, and its 颜色/大小/形状 is ...

**上边**

- In the top part of the image, we can see the 颜色/大小/形状 of ... is ....
- At the top of the image, we observe the 颜色/大小/形状 of ... is ....
- The top part of the image displays the 颜色/大小/形状 of ... is ....

**下边**

- ... is at the bottom of this graph，which is 颜色/大小/形状.
- At the bottom of the graph, we find ..., and its 颜色/大小/形状 is...
- ... is placed at the bottom of the graph, and we can see it is 颜色/大小/形状.

图片的最后可以像数据图/流程图一样，再次点明图片主题。

**图片题示范**

**[图片位置3：图片题示范 - 显示公园中两个人的场景]**

```text
The graph shows us two people in the park.

In the middle of the graph, I notice the color of the lady is red.

On the left part of the graph, we find that the color of the tree is green.

The top part of the image displays the size of the mountain is very large.

Grass is placed at the bottom of the graph, and we can see it is green.

In conclusion, this graph provides information about people in the park.
```
